Brainback

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective November 8, 2026

Brainback helps you block distracting apps and earn your screen time back. It works on your iPhone without an account. We do not sell personal information, run advertising or tracking SDKs, or upload your data to our servers.

Screen Time data

Brainback uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block the apps, categories and websites you choose and to show your screen time.

  • The apps and websites you pick are represented by private tokens from iOS. Brainback cannot see their names outside Apple's own views, and it cannot see your messages, browsing content or photos.
  • Screen time charts and top apps are drawn by Apple's Screen Time report inside the app. This usage data never leaves your iPhone and is never sent to us.
  • You can turn off Screen Time access for Brainback at any time in Settings > Screen Time, or by deleting the app.

Information stored on your device

Your blocks and schedules, unlock history, urges resisted, the reasons you choose before an unlock, focus sessions, Brain Score, streaks, badges, Rot Lock settings and preferences are stored locally on your device, in a private container shared only with Brainback's own widgets and extensions. A Buddy PIN is stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text.

Motion, notifications and sharing

  • The optional walking task asks for Motion & Fitness access to count steps on your device. Step counts are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications (for example a heads-up before a schedule starts, the end of a focus session or a trial reminder) are scheduled locally by iOS and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Rot Report images and invites are shared only when you tap Share, through the iOS share sheet, to the destination you pick.

Purchases and diagnostics

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may provide aggregated crash or diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it with developers in iOS settings.

Sharing, retention and deletion

We do not sell or share your data for advertising or tracking. Data stays on your device until you delete it in Brainback or remove the app. Purchase records kept by Apple are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Brainback has no accounts or social features and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Terms of Use

Use of Brainback is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed in your Apple ID settings.
