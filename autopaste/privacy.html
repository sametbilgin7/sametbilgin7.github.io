AutoPaste

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective October 26, 2026

AutoPaste is an on-device reusable-content workspace with an optional iOS custom keyboard. We do not sell personal information, run advertising SDKs, or upload your saved content to our servers.

Information stored on your device

Quick Messages, Information Cards, Response Flows, Saved Places, profile values used by Smart Fields, categories, favorites, recent usage, and preferences are stored locally on your device. AutoPaste does not require an account.

Custom keyboard and Full Access

The keyboard inserts an item only after you tap it. It does not choose recipients, press Send, send in the background, or record what you type. Built-in quick replies remain available without Full Access. If you optionally enable Full Access, the keyboard can read your saved AutoPaste items through the app's private App Group container. The developer does not receive your keystrokes, messages, recipients, or saved items.

Clipboard, location, and maps

  • Smart Import reads clipboard text only after you deliberately start that action.
  • Location permission is requested only when you choose to use your current location while creating a Saved Place.
  • Saved addresses and coordinates remain on your device. Apple MapKit may process map searches, previews, or directions under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases, notifications, and diagnostics

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive payment-card details. Optional local notifications are scheduled by iOS and can be disabled in Settings. Apple may provide aggregated crash or diagnostic information if you have enabled sharing with developers in iOS settings.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not sell or share saved content for advertising or tracking. Local content remains until you delete it in AutoPaste or remove the app. Purchase records retained by Apple are governed by Apple's policies.

Children's privacy

AutoPaste does not provide accounts or social features and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.
