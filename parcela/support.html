Common questions

How do I measure a field? Open Measure, choose Area, and tap each corner on the satellite map. You can also walk the boundary with GPS.

How accurate is a GPS walk? It depends on your iPhone's GPS, usually within 2–5 m under open sky. Hold the phone flat and walk at a steady pace.

Where are my measurements stored? On your iPhone, in the Saved tab. Export them as KML, GPX, GeoJSON, CSV or PDF to keep a copy elsewhere.

How do I restore Parcela Pro? Open the paywall or More and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription? In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Parcela.
