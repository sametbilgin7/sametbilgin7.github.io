Parcela Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Parcela: GPS Land Area Measure ("Parcela", "the app") measures land on a map. It is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Parcela has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. Nothing you measure is sent to the developer.

Location

With your permission, Parcela uses your iPhone's location to show where you are on the map and to record a boundary while you walk it. Location is processed on your device only. While a walk is recording, Parcela can keep using location when the screen is locked, so the boundary is not cut short. Location is never sent to the developer.

Your measurements and photos

Fields, notes, photos you attach and quotes you create are stored on your iPhone. They leave your device only when you choose to share or export them (for example as KML, PDF or CSV), and then only to the app or person you pick.

Apple services

Maps, place search and address lookup are provided by Apple Maps. When you search for a place or save a measurement, the search text or coordinates are sent to Apple to return results, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details.

Children

Parcela does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
